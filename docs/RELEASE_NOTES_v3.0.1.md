# Raikō 3.0.1

A small update to 3.0.

## Improved
- **Spotlight "For you" picks rotate.** Home's spotlight now shows a fresh mix of recommendations each
  time you start Raikō and every few hours — always mixing anime, movies and TV, and resting picks
  you've seen in the last day. News about your library still comes first.

## Fixed
- **Welcome tour scrolls to what it's showing.** Replaying the tour (Settings → About) with a page
  scrolled down now brings each highlighted element into view and keeps the whole step card on screen.
- The tour's overlay now follows the window when it's resized or maximized during the tour.
