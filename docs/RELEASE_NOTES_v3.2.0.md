# Raikō 3.2.0 — Themes, events and your music

**Make Raikō yours** — eight themes, festive and anime-world events that dress the app up, your favourite openings and endings liked, downloaded and ranked, and titles that move to Completed the moment you finish them.

## New

### 🎨 Themes
- **Eight themes** in **Settings → Appearance**: Raikō Night, Midnight Ink, Sakura Dawn, Neo-Tokyo, Studio Watercolor, Retro VHS, Zen Mono and High Contrast — each with its own colours and, for some, its own fonts and gentle effects (petals, scanlines…).
- **Make it yours:** pick an accent colour and a text size, and see any theme in a short **Preview** walkthrough before you switch.
- **Motion:** On, Calm (the default) or Off — and Raikō follows Windows' "Animation effects" setting. Effects stay light on your PC.
- Every theme is checked for readable contrast.

### 🎄 Events — Raikō dresses up for the occasion
- **New Year, Halloween and Christmas** each bring a look (with a few variations to choose from), decorations, Rai the little light spirit in costume and a hand-picked list of films and shows on Home.
- **Anime events from live data:**
  - **Season Premiere Week** (January, April, July, October) — the season's most anticipated shows lead Home.
  - **Premiere day** — when a highly anticipated show (or one on your list) airs episode 1: its characters around the banner, its official title logo and art in the spotlight, and a **cinema ticket** with its trailer.
  - **Farewell** — when a beloved series (or one you watch) airs its final episode: **end credits** with its cast and *Rank its songs*.
  - Shows on your list always come before more popular ones you don't follow.
- **A note from the team** for every holiday, written for the day.
- **The logo dresses up** — event colours and a Santa, witch or party hat, or the show's main character in its centre — and the line under the name says what's on.
- **Two events on one day** (say Halloween and a premiere): the holiday keeps the look, both get their banner and their moment.
- **You decide:** your own theme is never replaced without asking — events add decorations on top of it. Choose *Full*, *Decorations* or *Off* per event, *Ask me first*, or bring an event back, in **Settings → Appearance → Event themes**.

### 🎵 Your music
- A **Videos & music** section in every anime and TV detail panel: trailers, openings, endings and soundtracks.
- **♥ Like** the songs you love, **⬇ Download** them as MP3 (audio) or MP4 (video) to your Downloads folder, and **🏆 Rank** them — search any anime's openings to add them to your rankings.
- A new **Music** page (sidebar, or Ctrl+M): Liked, Rankings and Downloads.

### ✅ Finishing something moves it to Completed
- Watch the last episode of an anime that has finished airing, or of a TV show that has ended, and it moves to **Completed** (**Finished** for TV) by itself — with a toast and an **Undo** button.
- Shows that are still airing stay in Watching when you're caught up, since new episodes are coming.
- Anything you'd already finished that was still sitting in Watching is tidied up the first time you open 3.2.0 (one toast, one Undo).

### 🔀 Move titles between lists
- A status switcher in every detail panel: **Watchlist · Watching · Finished · Dropped** (Plan to watch / Completed for anime, Watched for movies) — only the moves that make sense are offered, with a short reason when one isn't (e.g. "Hasn't premiered yet").
- A running TV show you mark Finished goes back to Watching by itself when new episodes air.

### 🎉 Milestones
- 100 … 10,000 episodes, 10 … 500 movies, 10 … 250 series finished — each celebrated once with a toast and a little confetti.

## Improved
- Text that didn't fully fit in some places now shows completely, and the player's like / download buttons have more room.
- Toasts can now carry an action, like **Undo**.
- Raikō waits for a busy library instead of showing an error when two things save at the same moment.

Download **Raiko-v3.2.0-Windows.zip** below, unzip it and run `Raiko.exe` — or, if you already have Raikō 3.x, accept the update when Raikō offers it. Your library and settings are kept.
