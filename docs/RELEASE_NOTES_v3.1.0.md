# Raikō 3.1.0 — The Hall of Fame returns

**Your all-time favourites, ranked honestly and shown off beautifully** — plus notifications while Raikō is closed, smarter search and a cleaner look.

## New

### 🏆 Hall of Fame
- **Rebuilt from the ground up.** A new sidebar page with three wings, each its own ranking: **Miroku** (anime), **Movies** and **TV shows**. Your 10 rankings from the old Hall are restored in their original order.
- **"Which do you love more?"** Induct a title and Raikō asks a few head-to-head questions to find its exact place (about 4 questions for 10 titles, 7 for 100). Skip, *Too close to call* and *Undo* on every question, with keyboard shortcuts (← → Space S Backspace). Drag to fine-tune any time.
- **The Pantheon** — your four favourites of everything, at the top of the Hall and as a small strip on Home.
- **Five views:** Showcase, List (drag to reorder, ▲▼ movement), Tiers (S/A/B/C — drag between tiers, rename them), Timeline and Numbers (genres, studios, decades, hours).
- **Plaques:** click any entry for its story — your rating, time spent, when you watched it, best episode or season, rewatches — plus *why it's here*, your favourite moment and favourite character.
- **Share card:** export a PNG of your Pantheon, a Top 10, a tier list or your Honours (save or copy).
- **Connected everywhere:** detail panels show *★ #3 in your Miroku Hall* (or *Induct into Hall of Fame* for finished titles), rating a finished title 6/6 offers to induct it, and Hall of Fame titles count most in *For you*.

### 🎖️ Honours — your own awards
- Best trilogy, best duology, best saga, best season, best finale, best episode, best fight, best opening / ending, best villain, best protagonist, best character, best studio, best soundtrack, best of a year — or create your own.
- Each award has a winner, two runners-up and your reason.
- **Trilogies & sagas from anywhere:** search any series or collection (e.g. *The Lord of the Rings*) — found from TMDB collections and AniList sequel chains, even if it isn't in your library.
- **Character awards use the full cast** (every AniList character, the whole TMDB cast), most-loved first so the big villains and fan favourites are on top. Filter by name or by Main / Supporting.

### ➕ Add what you've watched, right from the Hall
- Every Hall picker also searches online. Pick a film, show or anime you've watched that isn't in Raikō yet and it's added to Kinema / Miroku as watched — then you carry on.

### 📅 This week, at a glance
- Clicking an entry in Home's *This week* opens a card right beside it: the episode (or SxxEyy with the episode's name and still), a live countdown, your progress, score, studio / network and runtime — for movies, the cinema / HD / disc dates — with *Open details* and *Trailer*.

### 🔔 Notifications when Raikō is closed
- Turn on **Settings → Notifications when Raikō is closed** and Windows tells you about new episodes, movies out in HD, new seasons, premiere dates and trailers — even with Raikō shut.
- Nothing keeps running in the background: Windows starts Raikō invisibly for a few seconds at each check (every 1–12 hours, after you log in and after your PC wakes), shows anything new, then closes it.
- Each event notifies once, clicking a notification opens that title, and Focus / Do Not Disturb is respected. There's a *Send a test notification* button.
- A **red badge on the taskbar** shows how many new things are waiting while Raikō is open.

### 🔎 Search that understands partial words
- Type "vin" and get Vinland Saga, "frie" → Frieren, "sev" → Severance, "oppen" → Oppenheimer, "jjk" → Jujutsu Kaisen — instantly, in both Discovers and Ctrl+K.
- Ctrl+K also suggests titles you don't have yet.

## Improved
- **New confirmation toasts** ("Episode 7 marked watched"): a solid card in the Raikō style with a coloured icon, clear text and a countdown line that pauses while you hover — no more see-through strip. Click it (or ✕) to dismiss.
- **Show more** at the end of every Discover list (Miroku and Kinema, including search results) loads the next page instead of stopping after the first few dozen.
- Opening Raikō while it's already open (or clicking a notification) brings the open window forward instead of starting a second copy.

## Install
Download **Raiko-v3.1.0-Windows.zip** below, unzip it and run `Raiko.exe` — or, if you already have Raikō 3.0, accept the update when Raikō offers it. Your library and settings are kept.
