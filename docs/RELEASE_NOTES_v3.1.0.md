# Raikō 3.1.0

## New
- **The Hall of Fame is back — rebuilt.** A sidebar page with three wings, each its own honest
  ranking: **Miroku** (anime), **Movies** and **TV shows**. Your 10 rankings from the old Hall are
  restored in their order.
  - **"Which do you love more?"** Induct a title and Raikō asks a few head-to-head questions to find its
    exact place (about 4 for 10 titles, 7 for 100). Skip, *Too close to call* and *Undo* on every
    question; keyboard ← → Space S Backspace. Drag rows to fine-tune any time.
  - **The Pantheon** — your four favourites of everything, at the top of the Hall and as a small strip
    on Home.
  - **Views:** Showcase (your #1 in full, #2–#3 as features, then the gallery), List (drag to reorder,
    ▲▼ movement), Tiers (S/A/B/C, drag between tiers, rename them), Timeline, and Numbers (genres,
    studios, decades, hours).
  - **Plaques:** click any entry for its story — rating, time spent, first watched → finished, best
    episode/season, rewatches — plus your *why it's here*, favourite moment and favourite character.
  - **Honours:** your own awards night — best trilogy, best duology (found for you from AniList sequel
    chains and TMDB collections, or put together by hand), best saga, best season, best finale, best
    episode, best fight, best opening/ending, best villain, best character, best studio, best
    soundtrack, best of a year, or your own — each with a winner, two runners-up and your reason.
  - **Share card:** a PNG of your Pantheon, a Top 10, a tier list or your Honours (save or copy).
  - Detail panels show *★ #3 in your Miroku Hall* (or *Induct into Hall of Fame* for finished titles);
    rating a finished title 6/6 offers to induct it (once). Hall of Fame titles weigh most in *For you*.
- **This week, at a glance.** Clicking an entry in Home's *This week* opens a card right beside it —
  episode (or SxxEyy and the episode's name and still), live countdown, your progress, score, studio /
  network, runtime; for movies the cinemas / HD / disc dates — with *Open details* and *Trailer*.
- **Notifications when Raikō is closed.** Turn on *Settings → Notifications when Raikō is closed* and
  Windows tells you about new episodes, movies out in HD, new seasons, premiere dates and trailers —
  even with Raikō shut. Windows starts Raikō invisibly for a few seconds at each check (every 1–12 h
  of your choice, after you log in, and after your PC wakes), shows a notification for anything new,
  then closes it. Nothing keeps running in between. Each event notifies once; clicking a notification
  opens that title; Windows Focus / Do Not Disturb is respected. There's a *Send a test notification*
  button.
- **Red badge on the taskbar** while Raikō is open: the number of new things waiting.
- **Search that understands partial words.** Type "vin" and get Vinland Saga, "frie" → Frieren,
  "sev" → Severance, "oppen" → Oppenheimer, "jjk" → Jujutsu Kaisen — instantly, in both Discovers and
  Ctrl+K. Results are ranked by how well the title starts with what you typed, then popularity. Ctrl+K
  also suggests titles you don't have yet.

## Changed
- Opening Raikō while it's already open (or clicking a notification) brings the open window forward
  instead of starting a second copy.
